## Research Areas

### Task-Metric Experience Graphs (TMEG) for Dynamic Manipulation
Solving the "static world" limitation of standard E-Graphs. I engineered TMEG, a framework that warps cached motion plans to dynamic constraints in real-time. By deriving Lipschitz-bounded heuristics from Weighted Jacobian Spectral Norms, this system guarantees search consistency and reduces planning times for 7-DOF manipulators from seconds to milliseconds.

### Flow Matching for Locomotion
Developing novel planning frameworks using flow matching techniques to generate smooth, natural locomotion trajectories for legged robots. Exploring how continuous normalizing flows can capture complex motion patterns and enable efficient trajectory generation for dynamic locomotion tasks.
