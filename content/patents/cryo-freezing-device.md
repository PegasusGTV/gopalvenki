Invented a novel cryogenic system for targeted ablation, engineering the complete mechanical and thermal architecture.

**Device Design:** Designed the complex mechanical assembly using SolidWorks and AutoCAD, featuring a custom vacuum-based insulation mechanism to maximize thermal efficiency and minimize energy loss.

**Simulation & Analysis:** Validated the design using COMSOL Multiphysics, simulating fluid flow and temperature distribution to ensure precise cryogen delivery.

**Material Engineering:** Conducted thermal stress analysis to select SS304 stainless steel, ensuring structural integrity and biocompatibility under extreme cryogenic conditions.
