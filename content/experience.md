## Previous Experience

### B.Tech Thesis - Device for Cryogenic Tumor Removal
**Guided by:** [Dr. Sarit K Das](https://www.iitm.ac.in/), Institute Professor and V. Balakrishnan Chair Professor, IIT Madras  
**Affiliation:** Indian Institute of Technology, Madras  
**Duration:** Sep 2022 - May 2024

Designed a cryosurgery device in **SolidWorks** and **AutoCAD** to eradicate bone tumors using liquid nitrogen. Implemented a vacuum-based insulation mechanism to minimize energy loss and enhance thermal efficiency. Simulated the device in **COMSOL Multiphysics**, analyzing fluid flow and temperature distribution during the cooling of bone tissue. Conducted thermo-fluidic performance validation to ensure effective tumor treatment. Selected **SS304 stainless steel** for its cryogenic properties and biocompatibility, ensuring safety and reliability for medical applications.
